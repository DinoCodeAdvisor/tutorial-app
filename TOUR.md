# Take a Tour of Dino-Forums

Dino-Forums is a small forum app built with Laravel. It gives people who want to share ideas a simple place to sign in, write posts, and join the conversation through comments.

Once you log in, you'll see your profile with your post and comment counts, along with a few posts to explore (or you can jump straight to a random one). From there you can create your own posts, comment on posts, edit or delete your own contributions, and restore them later if you change your mind.
