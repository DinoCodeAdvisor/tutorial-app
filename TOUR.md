Dino-Forums is a small, friendly discussion board built on Laravel where people can sign in and share short posts with the community. After logging in, each person lands on a profile-style homepage that shows their name, email, post and comment counts, along with a handful of randomly selected posts to browse. From there they can jump to a random post, open the post creation form, or head back to their profile.

The main flow is simple: sign in, skim the available posts, and open one to read it in full. On a post page, any signed-in user can add a comment, while the author also gets edit, delete, and restore controls for their own post; comments can likewise be edited or removed by the person who wrote them. Creating a post asks only for a short title and some content, and after saving, the author is taken straight to the new post so the conversation can begin.
